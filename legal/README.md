# GTA6 Shorts Bot

A personal automation tool run by Junaid (juneadiosdeveloper) for his own YouTube channel "GTA 6 Moments".

It finds popular public GTA 6 stream clips from creators who allow clipping, turns them into vertical Shorts with captions and credit to the original streamer, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

It is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts or services to the public.
