# Privacy Policy: GTA6 Shorts Bot

Last updated: 20 November 2026

GTA6 Shorts Bot is a personal tool used only by its owner, Junaid (GitHub: juneadiosdeveloper), to upload videos to his own YouTube channel.

**What data it accesses.** When the owner signs in with Google, the tool receives permission to upload videos and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API). It does not access any other Google data.

**How the data is used.** The access is used only to upload Shorts and set their titles, descriptions and privacy settings on the owner's channel.

**Storage.** The sign-in token is stored encrypted as a GitHub Actions secret in the owner's account. No personal data of other people is collected, stored, sold or shared.

**Third parties.** Data is not shared with anyone. The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

**Removing access.** The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/juneadiosdeveloper/science-shorts
