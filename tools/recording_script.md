# Paisa Science: recording scripts

## How to record
- Quiet room, fan and AC off. Phone a hand's width from your mouth.
- Read each line, then **pause 1 second** before the next line.
- One recording per video (for the long video: **one recording per chapter**, 10 files).
- Speak naturally, like explaining to a friend. You can change words, but **keep the numbers the same**.
- Made a mistake? Just record that video (or that chapter) again from the start. Short files make this easy.
- Send the files here in the chat. Name them if you can (e.g. "card", "infl", "ch1"...).

## Short 1: Credit card minimum due (6 lines)
1. Credit card ka sirf minimum due bharte ho? Ruko!
2. Minimum due bas dhai hazaar, par baaki par saadhe teen pratishat mahina byaaj!
3. Agar har baar sirf minimum bharte rahe, toh udhaar bahut dheere-dheere ghatta hai.
4. Khatam hone mein pandrah saal se zyada, aur byaaj lagbhag chauraanve hazaar!
5. Isliye hamesha poora bill bharo.
6. Minimum due, maximum byaaj!

## Short 2: Inflation (6 lines)
1. Ek lakh, bees saal baad sirf iktees hazaar?
2. Wahi saamaan har saal mehnga. Chhe pratishat par, sau ka saamaan baarah saal mein do sau ka.
3. Ek lakh ki taakat, das saal mein chhappan hazaar, bees saal mein iktees hazaar.
4. Asli kamaai, return minus mehngai. Saat mein se chhe gaya, bacha bas ek pratishat.
5. Paisa sirf jodo mat, mehngai se tez badhana seekho.
6. Mehngai, ek chhupa tax!

## Long video: SIP ki Poori Guide (10 chapters, one file each)

### Chapter 0: Shuruaat (6 lines)
1. Har mahine das hazaar rupaye. Bees saal. Aur aakhir mein, lagbhag ek crore?
2. Sunne mein jaadu lagta hai. Lekin yeh jaadu nahi, ganit hai.
3. Aaj is video mein hum SIP ka poora hisaab samjhenge.
4. SIP kya hai, yeh kaam kaise karti hai, compounding ka asli khel, SIP aur RD ka fark, samay ki taakat, step-up SIP, shuru kaise karein, aur woh paanch galtiyan jo logon ka lakhon ka nuksaan karwati hain.
5. Har number hum calculator se nikalenge, taaki aap khud check kar sako.
6. Ek zaroori baat: yeh video sirf samjhaane ke liye hai. Hum kisi fund ya stock ki salah nahi dete.

### Chapter 1: SIP kya hai (7 lines)
1. Chalo shuru karte hain. SIP aakhir hai kya?
2. SIP ka matlab hai, Systematic Investment Plan.
3. Yaani har mahine, ek tay tareekh ko, ek tay rakam, apne aap nivesh hoti rahe. Jaise recurring deposit, bas paisa bazaar se jude fund mein jaata hai.
4. Mutual fund ko ek tokri samjho. Usmein bahut saari companiyon ke shares ya bonds hote hain.
5. Aap jab paisa daalte ho, toh aapko us tokri ki units milti hain.
6. Ek unit ki us din ki keemat ko NAV kehte hain, yaani Net Asset Value.
7. SIP ki sabse achhi baat: aapko bazaar ka sahi samay pakadne ki zaroorat nahi. Aadat apna kaam karti hai.

### Chapter 2: Rupee cost averaging (10 lines)
1. Ab SIP ki pehli taakat samajhte hain: rupee cost averaging.
2. Maan lo aap har mahine ek hazaar rupaye daalte ho.
3. Pehle mahine NAV das rupaye hai, toh aapko sau units milti hain.
4. Doosre mahine bazaar gira, NAV aath rupaye. Ab utne hi paison mein ek sau pachchees units.
5. Teesre mahine bazaar chadha, NAV saadhe baarah rupaye. Ab sirf assi units.
6. Chauthe mahine phir das rupaye, yaani sau units.
7. Kul chaar hazaar rupaye, aur chaar sau paanch units.
8. Aapki average keemat padi lagbhag nau rupaye athaasi paise, jabki NAV ka average tha das rupaye terah paise.
9. Matlab, jab bazaar girta hai, SIP apne aap zyada units khareed leti hai. Girawat aapke liye sale ban jaati hai.
10. Lekin dhyaan rahe, yeh nuksaan se bachne ki guarantee nahi hai. Yeh bas khareedne ki keemat ko average kar deta hai.

### Chapter 3: Compounding ka hisaab (10 lines)
1. Ab SIP ki sabse badi taakat: compounding.
2. Compounding ka matlab hai, aapki kamaai par bhi kamaai.
3. Maan lo har mahine das hazaar, bees saal tak, aur saalana return baarah pratishat maan lete hain.
4. Aapki jeb se gaye kul chaubees lakh rupaye.
5. Aur bees saal baad, calculator ke hisaab se, yeh ban sakta hai lagbhag ninyaanve lakh nabbe hazaar.
6. Yaani lagbhag chhihattar lakh rupaye, sirf compounding ki kamaai.
7. Graph dekho. Pehle das saal line dheere chalti hai, phir tezi se upar bhaagti hai. Yahi compounding ka curve hai.
8. Par yaad rakho, baarah pratishat sirf ek maan liya gaya number hai.
9. Agar return das pratishat ho, toh rakam lagbhag chhihattar lakh saath hazaar. Aur aath pratishat ho, toh lagbhag unsath lakh.
10. Mutual fund ke return bazaar par nirbhar karte hain, aur koi bhi return pakka nahi hota.

### Chapter 4: SIP vs RD (7 lines)
1. Aksar log poochhte hain, SIP behtar hai ya recurring deposit?
2. Chalo wahi das hazaar mahina, bees saal ka hisaab lagaate hain.
3. Agar RD par saat pratishat byaaj maan lein, toh rakam banti hai lagbhag baawan lakh chaalees hazaar.
4. Aur SIP mein baarah pratishat maan kar, lagbhag ninyaanve lakh nabbe hazaar.
5. Lekin yeh tulna poori kahaani nahi hai. RD mein byaaj tay hota hai aur jokhim kam.
6. Equity fund ki SIP bazaar se judi hai. Kuchh saal achhe honge, kuchh saal nuksaan bhi dikhega.
7. Isliye sahi chunaav aapke lakshya, samay aur jokhim uthaane ki kshamata par nirbhar karta hai. Kam samay ke lakshya ke liye sthir vikalp, lambe lakshya ke liye log aksar SIP chunte hain.

### Chapter 5: Waqt ki taakat (7 lines)
1. Ab sabse zaroori cheez: samay.
2. Do dost hain, Amit aur Ravi. Dono har mahine das hazaar ki SIP karte hain, dono saath saal ki umar tak.
3. Amit pachchees saal ki umar mein shuru karta hai. Ravi das saal baad, paintees mein.
4. Amit ne kul lagaye bayaalees lakh. Ravi ne tees lakh.
5. Baarah pratishat maan kar, saath ki umar mein Ravi ke paas lagbhag ek crore nabbe lakh.
6. Aur Amit ke paas? Lagbhag chhe crore pachaas lakh!
7. Sirf baarah lakh zyada lagaye, lekin lagbhag saadhe teen guna rakam. Fark paise ka nahi, samay ka hai.

### Chapter 6: Step-up SIP (6 lines)
1. Ab ek smart tareeka: step-up SIP.
2. Har saal salary badhti hai, toh SIP bhi thodi badha do. Jaise har saal das pratishat.
3. Pehle saal das hazaar, doosre saal gyaarah hazaar, teesre saal baarah hazaar ek sau, aur aise hi. Beesven saal tak yeh lagbhag iksath hazaar mahina ho jaati hai.
4. Bees saal mein aapki jeb se jaayenge lagbhag adsath lakh sattar hazaar.
5. Aur baarah pratishat maan kar, rakam ban sakti hai lagbhag ek crore ninyaanve lakh.
6. Yaani saamaanya SIP se lagbhag doguni. Shuruaat chhoti rakho, bas har saal thoda badhaate raho.

### Chapter 7: SIP kaise shuru karein (8 lines)
1. Ab samajhte hain, SIP shuru karne ke aam kadam kya hote hain.
2. Pehla, aapka PAN aur KYC poora hona chahiye. Yeh ek baar ka kaam hai.
3. Doosra, lakshya aur samay tay karo. Paisa kitne saal baad aur kis liye chahiye.
4. Teesra, lakshya aur jokhim ke hisaab se fund ki category chuno. Lambe aur chhote lakshya ke liye alag soch hoti hai.
5. Chautha, SIP ki tareekh salary aane ke kuchh din baad rakho, taaki paisa hamesha khaate mein ho.
6. Paanchwa, auto-debit lagao, aur roz ki chinta chhod do.
7. Chhatha, saal mein ek baar review karo, aur salary badhe toh SIP bhi badhao.
8. Dhyaan rahe, hum kisi fund ka naam nahi sujhaate. Fund chunne se pehle uski jaankari padhein, ya registered salahkaar se baat karein.

### Chapter 8: 5 badi galtiyan (10 lines)
1. Ab woh paanch galtiyan, jo SIP ka fayda kha jaati hain.
2. Pehli galti: bazaar girte hi SIP band kar dena.
3. Maan lo bees saal ki SIP mein, paanchve saal bazaar gira, aur aapne ek saal SIP rok di.
4. Aapne sirf ek lakh bees hazaar kam lagaye, lekin aakhir mein lagbhag saat lakh sattar hazaar kam mile.
5. Aur girawat mein rukne ka matlab hai, sasti units khareedne ka mauka chhodna.
6. Doosri galti: roz-roz portfolio check karna. Lambi daud mein roz ka utaar-chadhaav bas shor hai.
7. Teesri galti: bahut saare fund. Das fund rakhne se diversification nahi, bas uljhan badhti hai.
8. Chauthi galti: kharche ko nazarandaaz karna. Har fund ek saalana fees leta hai, jise expense ratio kehte hain.
9. Agar kharche ki wajah se aapka return gyaarah ki jagah das pratishat reh jaaye, toh bees saal mein lagbhag das lakh assi hazaar ka fark pad sakta hai.
10. Paanchvi galti: bina lakshya ke SIP. Pehle tay karo, paisa kis liye hai aur kab chahiye. Ghar, bachchon ki padhaai, ya retirement.

### Chapter 9: Saar (8 lines)
1. Toh aaj ka saar.
2. SIP yaani har mahine anushaasan se nivesh.
3. Rupee cost averaging girawat mein zyada units dilaati hai.
4. Compounding ko samay chahiye, isliye jaldi shuruaat sabse bada fayda hai.
5. Step-up SIP se chhoti shuruaat bhi badi ban sakti hai.
6. Aur girawat mein ghabra kar SIP band mat karo.
7. Yeh video sirf jaankari ke liye hai. Nivesh se pehle apni research karein, ya SEBI registered salahkaar se baat karein.
8. Agar video kaam ka laga, toh like karo, channel ko subscribe karo, aur comment mein batao, aapne SIP kis umar mein shuru ki. Milte hain agle video mein.
